How much revenue could depend on departing accounts?

Scenarios, not predictions

Thesis to test: Intents revenue is only as durable as its solver base. This model tests a conditional exposure, not a proven causal relationship. Other makers may replace departing activity.

Model assumptions: no replacement or rerouting after exits; constant fee yield, capture fraction and price; and the sampled account-volume mix represents revenue-bearing activity. These assumptions are unverified. The model's lower loss bound is not a proven minimum loss: actual loss could be zero with replacement.

At-risk outputs measure incremental exposure to the selected departures after the assumed volume change. They exclude changes caused by the volume assumption itself. A 100% volume decline leaves no remaining flow exposed to an additional departure.

Observed verdict

Partial account attribution; operator independence unverified

The classifier attributes 15 executing accounts and 28.4% of sampled input USD. Model-free top-3 account share remains between 25.9% and 97.5%.

Use this evidence to decide whether a diversification claim is supported before relying on it in an assessment. An attribution gap does not establish a concentrated or fragile solver market.

What evidence would resolve this?

Authenticated settlement records

Link each sampled swap and intent hash to executed filler account deltas, amounts and timestamps. An unverified claim, receipt membership proof or quote is insufficient.

Operator mapping and disclosure coverage

Establish which accounts belong to independent operators. Preserve the full sampled USD denominator and all unknown volume; selective participation cannot establish market-wide diversification.

Scenario calculation

Let disclosed full-denominator shares be a₁ ≥ a₂ ≥ … and remaining share U = 1 − Σa. Top-N range: [Σ top-N a, min(1, Σ top-N a + U)]. HHI range: [Σa², (a₁ + U)² + Σ other a²]. At full disclosure the bounds meet; the resulting concentration can still be high.

Investment stance: no diversification premium from volume alone

Conditional research position

Do not use gross Intents swap volume as independently verified evidence of diversified, durable solver supply. This study supports withholding that assumption in a volume-driven bull case until settlement coverage and operator mappings make it testable. It does not establish a short position or a fair value for the NEAR token.

What would weaken this stance?

Authenticated records covering most sampled USD, independently mapped operators, and narrow concentration bounds showing diversified activity would weaken the auditability concern. Demonstrated continuity through independently documented maker exits would weaken a fragility hypothesis. Neither outcome is assumed. Growth, privacy and competition can coexist; privacy alone is not adverse evidence.
